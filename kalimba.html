About Kalimba Simulator

A 17-key kalimba (thumb piano) simulator with realistic physics-based audio synthesis.

How to play:

  • Click or tap any tine to pluck it and hear its note.
  • Drag across multiple tines to play a glissando.
  • The tines are arranged in the standard staggered layout: the center tine (C4) is the longest/lowest, with alternating left/right tines going up in pitch.
  • Notes span from C4 (middle C) to E6, covering over two octaves in C major.
