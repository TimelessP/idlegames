About Merge Game

The board starts filled with pieces. Merge them all down to a single tile to win!

How to play:

  • Click any empty cell to place the next piece from the queue there.
  • Click to select an occupied cell, then click an adjacent cell of the same level to merge them.
  • Alternatively, drag and drop a piece onto a matching neighbour.
  • Two same-level pieces merge into one at the next level up.
  • Two top-level pieces that merge both vanish — clearing them wins if the board is otherwise empty.
  • Goal: reduce the board to exactly one piece (or zero via a top-level merge).
  • You lose when no merges are left and the board is full.
